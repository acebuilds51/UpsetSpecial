Getting Started
Upset Special is a weekly college football pick'em league. Each week the commissioner posts a board of games with point spreads. You pick one team per game to win outright (the "upset special pick") and select straight winners for the rest. The player with the most points at the end of the regular season wins.
💡 The season runs alongside the college football regular season, typically Weeks 1–12/13.
The app works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — no app store needed.

iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  • Open the app in Safari (not Chrome)
  • Tap the Share button ⬆ at the bottom
  • Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • Tap Add
Android (Chrome):
  • Open the app in Chrome
  • Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right
  • Tap "Add to Home Screen" or look for an install banner at the bottom
  • Tap Add
💡 Once installed, the app opens full-screen like a native app and push notifications work automatically.
Enter your team name and PIN on the login screen. Your commissioner (Jeff) sets up your account and gives you your initial credentials. If you've forgotten your PIN, contact Jeff to reset it.
💡 Change your PIN right away via Profile → Change PIN. Use at least 4 digits.
Check the Getting Started checklist in your Profile tab. It walks you through:
  • Installing the app to your home screen

Making Picks
Picks open when the commissioner posts the weekly board, typically early in the week. The board shows all available games with point spreads. You can change your picks any time until the pick deadline, which is usually Thursday night or Friday morning before the first game kicks off.
Each week you designate one game as your Upset Special. For this game, instead of picking against the spread, you're picking the underdog to win outright. If the underdog wins, you earn bonus points equal to the point spread (so a +14 underdog winning earns you 14 extra points). If the underdog loses, you get nothing for that game.
💡 Choose wisely — a successful Upset Special can be the difference between first and second place.
The commissioner may apply no-pick defaults — typically giving you the favorite for games you missed. Check with Jeff about your league's specific no-pick policy. Enable push notifications so you never miss a deadline reminder.
Yes — you can change your picks as many times as you want until the pick deadline. Once the deadline passes (or games kick off), picks are locked.
After tapping Submit Picks, you'll see a green confirmation toast and your picks will appear in the "This Week" view. You can tap "This Week" in the bottom nav any time to review your current picks.
Scoring
Regular picks: 1 point for each correct pick against the spread.

Upset Special pick: If your underdog wins outright, you earn the point spread as bonus points (e.g., +21.5 spread = 21.5 bonus points). If they lose, 0 points for that game.

Perfect Week: If you go 10/10 on all picks in a week, it's recorded in your Trophy Room as a career achievement.
💡 The Upset Special pick is the highest-variance move in the game — most championships are won by players who hit a big upset.
For straight picks, you need the team you picked to cover the spread. If you pick a team listed at -7.5, they need to win by 8 or more. If you pick the underdog at +7.5, they need to lose by 7 or fewer — or win outright — for you to earn the point.
Tap Standings in the bottom navigation. You'll see the current season standings, week-by-week history, and your personal pick history. The 🏆 History tab shows all-time records across all seasons.
A Perfect Week means you went 10 for 10 — every single pick in that week was correct. It's one of the rarest achievements in the league. Perfect Weeks are recorded in your Trophy Room with a spinning bullseye trophy, and appear on the All-Time leaderboard.

Go to Profile → View My Trophy Room, or tap your name anywhere in the standings. Your Trophy Room shows your 3D spinning trophies for regular season finishes, Bowl Bonanza trophies, Perfect Week bullseyes, career stats, and your AI-generated bio.

Bowl Shield (Gold/Silver/Bronze) — Bowl Bonanza finishes

Account & Profile
Go to Profile → Change PIN. Enter your current PIN, then your new PIN twice to confirm. Your PIN must be at least 4 digits. If you've forgotten your current PIN, contact the commissioner to have it reset.
Go to Profile and tap your avatar image (or the placeholder circle). Your camera roll will open — select a photo and it will be compressed and saved automatically. Your avatar appears next to your name in the standings, chat, and Trophy Room.
The app must be installed on your home screen first. Then go to Profile → Notifications and tap Enable Push Notifications. Your browser will ask for permission — tap Allow. You'll get alerts when the weekly board is posted, when results are finalized, and for other league updates.
💡 Notifications only work when the app is installed as a PWA (added to home screen).
Team name changes are managed by the commissioner. Contact Jeff if you want to change your current team name. Note that your historical records are linked to your previous names — use the Name Claims feature to keep your history connected.
Go to Profile → Payment to find the commissioner's Venmo and PayPal handles. Mark your payment as sent in the app after paying — the commissioner will confirm receipt and mark your account as paid.
Technical & Troubleshooting
The app fetches live data from Google's servers, which can take 10–15 seconds on first load. Try:
  • Wait 15 seconds — a loading screen shows while data fetches
  • Check your internet connection
  • Force-close and reopen the app
  • Clear browser cache if using a browser (not the installed app)
💡 The installed PWA version is faster than the browser version.
Most errors are temporary (server hiccups). Try:
  • Wait 30 seconds and try again
  • Close and reopen the app
  • Check if your picks are saved before closing
If the error persists, contact Tony (the app builder) or Jeff with a screenshot.
Check these things in order:
  • The app is installed on your home screen (PWA) — not just a browser bookmark
  • Your phone's notification settings allow the app (check Settings → Notifications)
  • You enabled notifications in the app (Profile → Notifications)
  • Your device is not in Do Not Disturb mode
If still not working, try disabling and re-enabling notifications in the app.
After tapping Submit, you should see a green confirmation. If you don't see it:
  • Check your internet connection and try submitting again
  • Tap "This Week" to verify your picks appear
  • If the deadline has passed, your picks may be locked — contact Jeff
The app will show an update banner at the top when a new version is available. Tap it to reload. If you don't see the banner, try force-closing and reopening. On iOS, fully swipe the app away and reopen.
